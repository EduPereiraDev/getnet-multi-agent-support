# What is Getnet? About Getnet (O que é a Getnet, sobre a empresa)

Getnet is "The Leading AI Payments Company in Latin America and Europe", part of the Santander group (grupo Santander).

- #1 LATAM acquirer by purchase volume; #8 worldwide by purchase transactions.
- €238bn processed volume and 10.5bn transactions in 2025.
- 1.2M active merchants.
- Acquiring licence covering 35 countries.
- Presence in Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Uruguay, Colombia, Spain and Portugal.

## Our solutions (soluções)

- In-person payments (pagamentos presenciais).
- Online payments: Getnet SEP regional e-commerce API.
- Omnichannel.
- Value-added solutions.
- Agentic Commerce: payments initiated by AI agents; Getnet is a Mastercard Agent Pay partner. https://www.getnet.net/en/our-solutions/agentic-commerce
